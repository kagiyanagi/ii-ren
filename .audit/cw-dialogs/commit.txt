refactor(cw-dialogs): a tooltip that waits, and a divider that is gone

The dialog family against DESIGN.md 9's two recipes.

Tooltips, which is where the volume is -- 139 callers on StyledToolTip alone.
They appeared on the first pixel of hover, grew out of nothing (a size animated
on an effects spec, which reads as the scale the recipe forbids), and then the
QQC2 Basic style animated them again underneath, 300ms OutQuad in and the same
300ms InQuad back out. Now: a 500ms hover wait, one fade, 200 in and 130 out,
nothing that scales. StyledToolTipContent goes back to being a box -- both call
sites fade it from outside, so its own `shown`, `isVisible`, three Behaviors and
clip are gone with it. PopupToolTip gets the same wait and, for the first time,
an animation in either direction: its loader used to unmap the window the
instant the pointer left.

Dialogs: radius verylarge, the elevation 5 shadow the recipe has always asked
for, and an exit that is no longer the enter -- emphasizedAccel at half the
duration, with the body's fade on the effects specs and picked inside the
binding that writes it (2.9). Padding stopped being the container radius: it
came from Appearance.rounding, which sharp mode zeroes, so every dialog and
every DialogListItem row lost all of its padding the moment sharp mode was on.
16 now, which is also 5.2's number for a dialog. WindowDialogButtonRow fills its
width, so the filler Item that all seven callers already declare finally has
width to take and the confirming action sits at the right edge instead of the
left. Its Layout.margins: -8 -- negative margins, design law 11 -- is deleted.

WindowDialogSeparator dies. Zero callers, 5.5 forbids it, and SelectionDialog
was hand-rolling two more of them around its list: those become one card, the
way the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth dialogs already do it. WindowDialogSectionHeader and
WindowDialogParagraph take the whitespace half of that replacement, and both
they and the title now fill their width, without which Text.Wrap did nothing.

Finding 1, the focus layer, lands on ShortcutBox -- a raw MouseArea that had no
focus state, no pressed state, and a hover painted with colSecondaryContainer-
Hover, which is the 0.10 mix, so its hover rendered at the pressed token's
strength. One StateOverlay does all three at the right opacity, plus tab focus
and Space/Return. DialogListItem inherits its four states from RippleButton and
only needed its state film pointed at layer 3.

NoticeBox is the shell's failure surface and every one of them looked like a
neutral notice. It reads the severity off the Material Symbol the callers
already pass, so the three that say "error" say it in colError without 38 call
sites repeating a flag, and a notice with nothing to say now hides itself
instead of drawing an empty coloured slab.

tools/check-dialog-surfaces.py holds the contract: the delay, the fade-only
rule, both tooltip transitions from named specs, the popup outliving its own
condition, the dialog's scrim/shadow/radius/half-duration exit, no padding from
a rounding token, no hairlines, and a focus film on anything answering a click.
Mutation-tested against eleven single-line regressions.
