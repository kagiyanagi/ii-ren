refactor(cw-config-rows): a film that reaches its card, and a drag that lifts

Twelve widgets, and almost all of modules/settings is six of them in a column.

ContentGroup hands a row the card's four corner radii and its 8px per-side
bleed only if the row exposes `buttonRadius`. ConfigSwitch is rooted in
RippleButton and got them; nothing else in the family did. So the search flash
on a spin box was anchored -2/-2/-4/-4 -- four pixels short of its card
sideways and two pixels *past* it top and bottom, which is 10.13 word for word
-- and ConfigTextField painted a rounding.verysmall rectangle of its own that
was harmless only because its colour was transparentize(..., 1), alpha zero.
Both now declare buttonRadius purely so the handshake fires, and read the
card's geometry back out of it. That also makes the propagation reach through
ConfigRow (5.6): a spin box in a row now takes only the edges it touches.

Finding 1 mattered most here and this is where it landed: ConfigTextField had
no focus state at all -- 3.7's "an activeFocus boolean nothing renders" --
and now composites the 0.10 film; MonitorRect had hover only and no keyboard
at all, and now has four states, activeFocusOnTab and Enter/Space to select.

3.6 twice: a dragged bar component faded to 0.8 opacity on elementResize, a
spatial spec that overshoots past 1 (the family's only check-design hit), and
a dragged display dropped to 0.7 alpha. Both lift now -- the 0.16 drag film
plus a scale -- and neither takes a shadow, because one is a repeated delegate
(8, 10.11) and the other would have to restructure MonitorCanvas to get a
sibling to cast it.

5.7 on every label that could outgrow its cell: ConfigSwitch's (129 callers),
ConfigTextField's, the bar entry's title, and the fixed-60px "Style" cell in
the spacebar drawer. ConfigSlider's label-above-full-width-track was already
right; what it was missing was Layout.fillWidth, which 35 of its 124
instantiations set by hand while the other 89 sized to their label row inside
a full-width card.

Also: ConfigSwitch dimmed its icon and label a second time on top of
RippleButton's, so a disabled row's text sat at 0.16 while its switch sat at
0.4; ConfigSelectionArray overrode GroupButton's disabled 0.4 with 0.5;
ConfigListViewEntry declared colHover and never used it, and coloured its
title colOnLayer0 on a layer-2/3 surface; ConfigListView's root was a
transparent Rectangle carrying a rounding.large that painted nothing; and
ConfigSubPageHost slid in and out on the same 500ms spatial spec, whose
overshoot could push x past the width that releases overlayActive.

No row-level dividers or separator bars exist in this family; 5.5 had nothing
to kill. ConfigListView is the only widget here that can be empty and it
already collapses to the one control that fixes it, so no PagePlaceholder.

tools/check-config-row-cards.py asserts both halves of the ContentGroup
handshake, because these films are at opacity 0 except during the three blinks
of a search hit -- break one and nobody sees it until somebody searches for
that setting.
