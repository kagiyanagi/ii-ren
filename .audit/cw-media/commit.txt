refactor(cw-media): a lyric stack that finally reads real lyrics, and a transport that dims without a player

Lyrics.qml was quietly dead: it branched on LyricsService.status and indexed
LyricsService.slots/before, none of which exist on the service (verified by
reading LyricsService.qml and its LrclibLyrics child in full) -- so its "ok"
branch never rendered and its retry tap, plus PlayerControlsLyrics' seek
handler, called a restartLyrics() that was never defined anywhere. Rewired
both branches onto the service's real hasSyncedLines/currentIndex/
syncedLines/statusText using the same windowed-index pattern LyricScroller
already uses, added a real (documented no-op) restartLyrics(), and swapped
the bare MouseArea retry tap for a RippleButton so it inherits a focus ring
and a press separate from hover for free. The fallback branch now shows
statusText as one line instead of a spinner that could never turn off --
closes this family's "no lyrics" edge state.

Elsewhere: tokenized LyricScroller's only hand-fit animation (400ms OutQuart
-> elementMoveSmall/expressiveFastSpatial) and Lyrics.qml's opacity Behavior
(-> elementMoveFast) for finding 3; snapped PlayerControls.qml and
PlayerControlsLyrics.qml's off-grid margins/spacing, PlayerControlsLyrics'
literal card radius, and its two hex-literal sentinel checks (now
Qt.colorEqual, which also survives however AdaptedMaterialScheme's black
serialises) for the densest hit pair in the tranche; grew both files'
TrackChangeButton from 24px to the 32px minimum hit area; gave Player.qml's
unset card radius a real default; and dimmed MaterialMusicControls via one
enabled binding on its ButtonGroup when MprisController has no active player,
relying on GroupButton's own already-tokenized disabled opacity rather than
adding a second one.

LyricsStatic.qml was not deleted -- it has one live caller (bar/Media.qml)
and neither Lyrics nor LyricScroller is a drop-in replacement for it as they
stand; the migration is written up in notes.md instead. LyricScroller's
visible-when-no-lyrics gate was checked against all three of its callers and
left alone: two of them depend on it collapsing so their own fallback shows.
